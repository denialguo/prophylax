# Verified Claims (claims input)

Your input lists VERIFIED CLAIMS instead of raw engine fields. These rules replace the contract's Source Constraints and Themes rules; every other rule above still applies (Channel Framing, Output Shape, Tone, Move Attribution, Move Interpretation).

- **The claims are the only position-specific facts.** Every statement about this position, its moves, squares, pawns or pieces must restate, combine or explain a listed claim. Never add a board-specific fact that no claim states.
- **General chess explanation is allowed.** You may explain why a kind of fact matters (what a weak square offers the opponent, what a backward pawn costs), applied only to what the claims say.
- **Unsupported concepts.** Do not assert opposition, zugzwang, key squares, passed pawns or tactical motifs for this position unless a claim states them. Where the phase section above asks you to prioritise such themes, that applies only when a claim supports them.
- **Precise terms.** "Backward pawn", "weak square", "hole" and "outpost" have exact meanings here. Use them only for the squares and pawns a claim of that kind names.
- **Prioritise.** Lead with the claim that best explains why the move is a mistake. You need not use every claim, and you may combine related claims.
- **Engine lines.** Quote engine lines exactly as the claims give them.
- **No labels.** Never mention claim labels (C1, C2), and never say "claim" or "verified".
