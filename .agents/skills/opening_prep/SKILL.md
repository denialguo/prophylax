# Analysing Openings - Narration Rules

Loaded after `../narration_contract.md`, which applies in full.

## Phase Interpretation
In the opening phase, narrate the feature changes and concessions according to the following principles:
- **Development & Tempo**: Value structural commitments (such as pawn moves) strictly against the loss of development tempo. Pushing pawns at the expense of piece mobilization should be highlighted as a concession of the initiative.
- **Center Control**: Frame pawn push concessions around the struggle for control of the central squares.
- **King-in-Center Risk**: Frame king-safety deltas around the vulnerability of an uncastled king stuck in the center of the board.
- **WDL Refutations**: Narrate WDL drops and tactical refutations (`refutation_pv`) as immediate developmental punishment—demonstrating how the opponent exploits the lag in piece mobilization or center abandonment.
- **Vocabulary (1800+)**: use directly (e.g., development lead, structural commitment, tempo, space advantage) without definitions.
